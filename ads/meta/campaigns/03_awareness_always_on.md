# Campaign: Always-On Brand Awareness — Oswego & CNY

## Why This Campaign
OCH has no active top-of-funnel campaign keeping the brand visible between events. The viral post did this accidentally — this campaign does it intentionally, cheaply, and consistently. Goal: keep OCH in the feed of locals and CNY visitors so event and retargeting campaigns convert easier.

## Content Rotation Strategy

Rotate one new piece of content per week from the categories below. Pull from existing social content where possible — this campaign amplifies what's already working organically.

### Category 1 — Food Porn (highest CTR)
Short video or photo of a hero item: brisket slice, loaded mac, pulled pork sandwich.

**Caption formula:**
> [One-line sensory hook about the food]
>
> Slow smoked. Worth the drive. Old City Hall, Oswego NY.

---

### Category 2 — The Vibe / Atmosphere
Photo or video of the space, bar, waterfront, or a busy Friday night.

**Caption formula:**
> [Reference to Oswego or the community]
>
> This is what a Friday night is supposed to look like. OCH, Oswego.

---

### Category 3 — Beer Feature
Highlight a rotating tap, a new seasonal brew, or the brewery itself.

**Caption formula:**
> [Beer name or style] is on tap.
>
> Brewed local. Poured cold. Come get one.

---

### Category 4 — Community/Story
A behind-the-scenes moment, a staff feature, or a throwback to the building's history.

**Caption formula:**
> [Oswego/CNY pride angle]
>
> Old City Hall has been part of this community for [X] years. We're not going anywhere.

---

## Retargeting Pipeline
Everyone who watches 50%+ of any video in this campaign gets added to the warm retargeting audience used in Campaign 01 (Event Space Leads). This campaign feeds that pipeline automatically.
