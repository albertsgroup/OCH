# Old City Hall Barbecue & Brewery — Brand Reference

## Brand Voice

- **Mix**: 75% BBQ / 25% beer
- **Personality**: Approachable, confident, smokehouse storyteller, community-first
- **Humor**: Oswego humor — dry, self-aware, locally grounded
- **Setting**: Downtown waterfront hangout
- **Primary Audience Age**: 30–55
