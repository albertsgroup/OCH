# Old City Hall Barbecue & Brewery — Brand Reference

> **IMPORTANT:** Read this file before generating any content, copy, captions, ads, or creative for Old City Hall Barbecue & Brewery. Apply this brand voice to every output without being asked.

---

## Brand Voice

- **Mix**: 75% BBQ / 25% beer
- **Personality**: Approachable, confident, smokehouse storyteller, community-first
- **Humor**: Oswego humor — dry, self-aware, locally grounded
- **Setting**: Downtown waterfront hangout
- **Primary Audience Age**: 30–55

---

## Tone of Voice

Approachable, clever, confident, a wink of Oswego humor, and locally grounded with a smokehouse storyteller's voice.

**Tone anchor:** Think "Simple Man" by Lynyrd Skynyrd — not flashy, earned, real.

---

## Brand Values

- Community roots
- Slow-crafted food and beer worth gathering for
- Quality time around the table
- Oswego / Syracuse / CNY pride
- Historic place with modern flavor
- Unpretentious authenticity

## Visual Style Guidelines

### What's Approved
- Warm, amber/golden tones — natural light, fire light, pendant bar light
- Dark backgrounds (dark wood, slate, black) with product sharply in focus
- Bold serif or slab typography — heavy, confident, earned
- Minimal props — let the food and beer do the talking
- OCH logo badge (white or cream) anchored bottom-right or bottom-center
- Hop cone icon used as a decorative accent
- Weathered textures: reclaimed wood, brick, cast iron

### What's NOT Approved (off-brand)
- Bright white or sterile backgrounds
- Trendy minimalist sans-serif typography
- Stock-photo-generic imagery
- Overly polished or "fine dining" aesthetics
- Neon or fluorescent color schemes
- Content that feels like a chain restaurant
