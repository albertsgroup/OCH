# Q3 & Q4 2026 Marketing Goals — Old City Hall Barbecue & Brewery

Covers August–December 2026. Every month runs three channels (SEO, Engagement, Ads) against a mix of always-on themes (Catering + To-Go, Stein Society) and seasonal themes (Fall in Aug–Oct, Christmas in Nov–Dec). See `brand/OCH_brand.md` for voice before drafting any copy against these goals.

## Changes made from the original draft

Two gaps in the draft outline broke the pattern the other months follow — fixed here for consistency:

1. **October SEO was missing Fall.** August and September both run Fall across all three channels, and October still has it under Engagement and Ads — just dropped from SEO. Added back in, since October is peak fall-beer season.
2. **November and December Ads were missing Stein Society.** SEO and Engagement carry it every month including Nov/Dec, but Ads dropped it those two months. Added back in — Stein Society membership ($100/year) is a strong Christmas gift angle, and year-end is a natural renewal push.

## Theme playbook

Reusable per-theme goals — the monthly checklist below just flags which themes are live each month.

### Catering + To-Go (every month, Aug–Dec)
- **SEO:** Keep Google Business Profile and site copy optimized for "BBQ catering Oswego County" / "BBQ to-go near me" and similar local intent terms.
- **Engagement:** Behind-the-scenes catering setup posts, customer catering photos, to-go order highlights.
- **Ads:** Geo-targeted campaigns promoting catering packages and to-go ordering; retarget past catering inquiries.

### Stein Society — mug club (every month, Aug–Dec)
- **SEO:** Keep a dedicated Stein Society landing page optimized for "mug club Oswego" / "brewery membership CNY."
- **Engagement:** Spotlight member perks, feature member stories, promote member-only events.
- **Ads:** Membership sign-up campaigns built around the $100/year perks; retarget engaged fans and past visitors.

### Fall (Aug–Oct)
- **SEO:** Optimize for fall beer release and seasonal-event search terms (e.g. "fall beer CNY," "Oswego fall events").
- **Engagement:** Tease fall beer drops and tasting notes, promote booking for fall parties and local events.
- **Ads:** Promote fall beer releases plus catering for fall parties, tailgates, and local events.

### Christmas (Nov–Dec)
- **SEO:** Optimize for "Christmas restaurant Oswego," "holiday dining CNY," and similar destination-holiday terms.
- **Engagement:** Behind-the-scenes decorating reveal (20 trees, ceiling ornaments), countdown content, encourage guest photo tagging.
- **Ads:** Drive visits to OCH as a Christmas destination; promote Stein Society membership as a gift; catering for holiday parties.
