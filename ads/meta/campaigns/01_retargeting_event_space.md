# Campaign: Event Space Retargeting — Oswego Viral Post Warm Audience

## Why This Campaign
The "Oswego, it's official" post reached 22,010 people and generated 14,752 thruplays at $0.06 each — your warmest, cheapest-to-build audience. Those people already know OCH. The previous lead gen campaigns spent $898 targeting cold audiences and got 14 leads at $64 each. This campaign flips that: hit warm engagers with a specific event space offer.

## Notes
- This outperforms cold lead gen because the audience already has a positive association with OCH from the viral video.
- Once warm audience is exhausted (~3–4 weeks), layer in a 1% lookalike audience built from the same engagers.
- Rotate creatives every 3–4 weeks to prevent fatigue.
